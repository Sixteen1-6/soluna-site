From the founders

A paddle you keep
deserves a home
worth keeping.

Soluna started in Lexington with a simple feeling: the good days on court are worth dressing for. We couldn't find gear that matched that feeling, so we made it. The Case came first, built to hold a paddle the way a watch box holds a watch: structured, considered, and quietly proud. We made Soluna because the game already gives you the joy. Your gear should be worthy of it.
